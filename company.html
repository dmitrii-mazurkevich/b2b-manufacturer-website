About the company

A family business that grew from a repair workshop into a full-cycle plant: design, casting, machining, assembly and testing in one place.

History

  1. 1998Valve repair workshop with six people.
  2. 2004First own series: cast iron gate valves for water utilities.
  3. 2011Own foundry and a test bench up to PN 160.
  4. 2017ISO 9001 and first exports, ball valves for oil and gas.
  5. 2023Electric actuators of our own design, deliveries to 40 countries.
